# Results Summary

## Descriptive results

Condition means are averages of the ten measure-level means; each measure-level mean averages four trials.

- Single agent: claim-weighted preservation 0.8803; component-weighted preservation 0.8792; mean distortion severity 0.0148; any-distortion rate 0.0138; material-distortion rate 0.0010.
- Independent ensemble: claim-weighted preservation 0.8929; component-weighted preservation 0.8863; mean distortion severity 0.0211; any-distortion rate 0.0183; material-distortion rate 0.0028.
- Normal debate: claim-weighted preservation 0.8902; component-weighted preservation 0.8849; mean distortion severity 0.0238; any-distortion rate 0.0238; material-distortion rate 0.0000.
- Critic debate: claim-weighted preservation 0.9214; component-weighted preservation 0.9201; mean distortion severity 0.0289; any-distortion rate 0.0269; material-distortion rate 0.0020.

## Planned paired contrasts and statistical uncertainty

- Independent − single: mean paired difference 0.0126, 95% measure-bootstrap CI [-0.0002, 0.0260]. Measure directions: 5 positive, 2 negative, 3 zero. LOMO range [0.0088, 0.0159]; direction changed: no.
- Normal debate − independent: mean paired difference -0.0027, 95% measure-bootstrap CI [-0.0096, 0.0040]. Measure directions: 4 positive, 5 negative, 1 zero. LOMO range [-0.0045, -0.0007]; direction changed: no.
- Critic debate − normal debate: mean paired difference 0.0313, 95% measure-bootstrap CI [0.0053, 0.0634]. Measure directions: 7 positive, 1 negative, 2 zero. LOMO range [0.0188, 0.0363]; direction changed: no.

## Distortion findings

All three frozen distortion outcomes were retained separately; no composite distortion endpoint was constructed.

- Independent − single: mean-severity difference 0.0063, 95% CI [-0.0090, 0.0206]; any-distortion-rate difference 0.0045, 95% CI [-0.0108, 0.0185]; material-distortion-rate difference 0.0018, 95% CI [0.0000, 0.0044].
- Normal debate − independent: mean-severity difference 0.0027, 95% CI [-0.0055, 0.0123]; any-distortion-rate difference 0.0055, 95% CI [-0.0024, 0.0144]; material-distortion-rate difference -0.0028, 95% CI [-0.0068, 0.0000].
- Critic debate − normal debate: mean-severity difference 0.0050, 95% CI [-0.0043, 0.0140]; any-distortion-rate difference 0.0030, 95% CI [-0.0057, 0.0119]; material-distortion-rate difference 0.0020, 95% CI [0.0000, 0.0060].

## Heterogeneity and sensitivity

Measure-level effects were heterogeneous, especially for critic debate versus normal debate, where seven measures were positive, one negative, and two tied. Despite this heterogeneity, the claim-weighted LOMO estimate remained positive under every omission. The independent-versus-single and normal-versus-independent claim-weighted estimates also retained their respective directions under every one-measure omission, although both bootstrap intervals included zero.

## Interpretation boundaries

These are paired condition comparisons from a controlled repeated-trial design. Confidence intervals resample the ten measures, which are the inferential units. No component-level independence or p-value testing was used. Unsupported-claim and word-count outcomes were not calculated because no complete frozen locked unsupported-claim input was available.
