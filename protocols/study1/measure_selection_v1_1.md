# CA-TradeoffBench Confirmatory Measure Selection v1.1

## Selection Rule

The confirmatory benchmark includes every California statewide proposition
that appeared in statewide elections during calendar year 2024, excluding
Proposition 35.

Proposition 35 is excluded because it was previously designated as M01,
the development measure, and its outputs were inspected during benchmark
and protocol development.

California voters considered:

- Proposition 1 in the March 5, 2024 Presidential Primary Election
- Propositions 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 32, 33, 34, 35, and 36 in the
  November 5, 2024 General Election

After excluding Proposition 35, ten untouched measures remain.

This creates the complete confirmatory set M02-M11.

## Confirmatory Measures

M02 — Proposition 1
Authorizes $6.38 Billion in Bonds to Build Mental Health Treatment
Facilities for Those with Mental Health and Substance Use Challenges;
Provides Housing for the Homeless.

M03 — Proposition 2
Authorizes Bonds for Public School and Community College Facilities.

M04 — Proposition 3
Constitutional Right to Marriage.

M05 — Proposition 4
Authorizes Bonds for Safe Drinking Water, Wildfire Prevention, and
Protecting Communities and Natural Lands from Climate Risks.

M06 — Proposition 5
Allows Local Bonds for Affordable Housing and Public Infrastructure
with 55% Voter Approval.

M07 — Proposition 6
Eliminates Constitutional Provision Allowing Involuntary Servitude
for Incarcerated Persons.

M08 — Proposition 32
Raises Minimum Wage.

M09 — Proposition 33
Expands Local Governments' Authority to Enact Rent Control on
Residential Property.

M10 — Proposition 34
Restricts Spending of Prescription Drug Revenues by Certain Health
Care Providers.

M11 — Proposition 36
Allows Felony Charges and Increases Sentences for Certain Drug and
Theft Crimes.

## Rationale

The measure set is defined by a complete calendar-year inclusion rule rather
than by anticipated model behavior, policy complexity, expected treatment
effects, or researcher preference.

No confirmatory measure was selected because it was expected to favor or
disfavor any experimental condition.

The set naturally includes multiple policy domains and multiple measure types,
including statutes, constitutional amendments, bond measures, fiscal policy,
health policy, housing, labor, criminal justice, education, and environmental
policy.

## Confirmatory Rule

No experimental outputs for M02-M11 may be evaluated before the corresponding:

1. official source packet,
2. high-level reference inventory, and
3. atomic-component inventory

have been completed and frozen.

The measure set itself is frozen before confirmatory model generation.
